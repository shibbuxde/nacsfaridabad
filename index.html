The NACS Method

How a chapter becomes a result

STEP ONE

Learn in small batches

Limited seats per batch, so doubts are asked and answered in the same class — not carried home.

STEP TWO

Write, don't just read

Accounts is a writing subject. Students practise full answers by hand, the way boards actually mark them.

STEP THREE

Test every week

Short weekly tests, checked copies returned with remarks, and a revision cycle before every exam.
